Breathing Index is a logbook lens on public air data — not medical advice. Trust your symptoms and your asthma action plan over anything on this screen.

What this is

A free tool that shows public air quality data and predicts a 1–4 rating from your own breathing logs. It is informational. It is not a medical device, not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for a clinician who knows your lungs. Nothing here is a doctor–patient relationship.

If breathing feels dangerous, use your rescue plan and get help, whatever this app says. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

The data is not guaranteed

Readings come from Open-Meteo (model output) and AirNow (stations), and I do not control, verify, or vouch for either. Model data misses hyper-local smoke. Stations can be miles away. Averaging windows lag reality. The forecast on top of all that is an inference from a handful of your own taps, and on day one it is population breakpoints with your name nowhere in it. Treat every number here as a hint that could be wrong.
